What initiated Biobuild 1.0?

We believed in community building and we recognize the need to remain committed to our vision as an association; advancing biomedical engineering for proper healthcare in Nigeria, even as students. Then as the president, I come about this initiative that helps both the students growth and also advance the field.

The BioBuild initiative, to create the building or innovation culture among students, solve pressing healthcare issues with tech in Nigeria and bring the association to assume it's responsibility for global impact while supporting the Academic success of the newer students to sustain early good academic standing.

We are making this real through an intense Hackathan engaging Summit, healthcare outreach to secondary schools and scholarship opportunities for outstanding 200lvl and 100lvl students.

We pioneered another initiative to engage students and experts all over the world, to interact together about the possibilities behind this our standpoint and to share values that cam encourage healthcare innovation and advancement among the students or graduates. The initiative is called BioBuild Accelerator Series, featuring a webinar and a podcast.

However, I see NABETS FUTA producing the next gen healthcare builders and I strongly believe the coming years would prove what a community of vibrant students can achieve together. Thank you.

Comr.Opeyemi Olawuwo
President, 2026/2027 NABETS FUTA.

The BioBuild Summit

The BioBuild Summit is a premier gathering of healthcare leaders, technology innovators, and emerging engineering talent coming together to shape the future of medical technology. Serving as a thought-leadership platform, the summit fosters critical conversations around healthcare infrastructure, local innovation, and industry partnerships, inspiring attendees to drive sustainable clinical impact while creating a direct bridge between academic research, regulatory frameworks, and commercial viability.

The BioBuild Hackathon

Think. Collaborate. Create. Innovate.

The BioBuild Hackathon is an intensive innovation arena where creative minds converge to engineer practical solutions to some of healthcare’s most pressing challenges. It bridges the gap between academic theory and real-world impact, empowering participants to transform bold ideas into functional prototypes that can improve patient care and clinical workflows.

Through teamwork and cross-disciplinary collaboration, participants will identify real-world problems, brainstorm innovative ideas, and develop practical, scalable solutions using technology, engineering, and creativity. The hackathon also provides an opportunity to build valuable networks, strengthen technical skills, and contribute to the advancement of healthcare innovation across Nigeria and beyond.

Don't just identify problems. Come together, build solutions, and make an impact.

The BioBuild Scholarship Programme

Recognizing Excellence. Rewarding Potential. Inspiring Growth.

The BioBuild Scholarship Programme is an initiative designed to recognize academic excellence, encourage healthy competition, and motivate students to strive for outstanding performance.

200-level students: the scholarship award will be based on academic performance, with the student who achieves the highest CGPA qualifying for the award, subject to the programme's selection criteria.

100-level students: an assessment examination will be organized by the BioBuild committee for registered participants. The top three students with the highest scores will receive scholarship awards in recognition of their performance.

Through this initiative, BioBuild seeks to celebrate outstanding students, encourage academic dedication, and inspire a culture of excellence within the Biomedical Engineering community.
